Cleaning tips

Move-out cleaning checklist: room by room

Bay Area Shine · October 2026 · 2 minute read

Handing back the keys goes smoother when the place is spotless. Use this checklist whether you clean yourself or book a move-out clean. Every item on it is part of our move-out clean.

Before cleaning day

  • Move everything out first. An empty home is faster to clean, and nothing gets missed behind boxes.
  • Take out what trash and recycling you can.
  • Keep the lights and water on until the clean is done.
  • Sort out access: a door code or a key.

Kitchen

  • Counters, sink and stovetop
  • Microwave, inside and out
  • Inside the oven
  • Inside the fridge
  • Inside the cabinets
  • Cabinet and drawer fronts
  • Outside of the fridge, oven and dishwasher

Bathrooms

  • Toilet, tub or shower, sink and mirrors
  • Tile and grout scrubbed
  • Sanitized, ready for first use

Bedrooms and living areas

  • Surfaces dusted
  • Closets and shelves wiped out
  • Inside windows
  • Door frames, light switches and vents
  • Baseboards

Floors and the final walkthrough

  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash taken out
  • Ready for the final walkthrough

What a move-out clean doesn't cover

We don't move heavy furniture, wash outside windows, or clean mold, biohazards or pest messes. Ask if you're not sure.
