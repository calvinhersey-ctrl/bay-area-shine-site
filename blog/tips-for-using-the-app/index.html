Using the app

10 tips for using the Bay Area Shine app

Bay Area Shine · October 2026 · 3 minute read

The app is in testing now. These tips are for beta testers, and for everyone once it opens.

A few small habits make every clean go smoother. Here are ten ways to get the most from the app.

1. Set up your home once

Add your address, home size, rooms, pets, the products you like or want avoided, parking and how to get in. It takes about two minutes, and every clean after that starts from it.

2. See the price before you pay

Pick your clean, your home size and any extras, and the app shows the price before you pay. The $40 deposit holds your time and comes off your total (there's no deposit on our plans).

3. Choose "Around then" or "Exactly then"

You pick the day and the start time. "Around then" means your cleaner arrives within an hour either way. "Exactly then" means at that time. Once a cleaner is chosen, we confirm their arrival time.

4. Choose your cleaner, or let us pick

Cleaners apply for your clean. Choose one yourself, or let us pick for you. Either way, you always see who's coming before the day: their first name, photo, rating and reviews. If your cleaner brings a helper, you see them too, and you can say no up to 24 hours before.

5. Keep your door code in the locked field

If you won't be home, put your door or gate code in the app's locked code field, never in a chat or a text. Only your cleaner (and any helper on your clean) sees it, from an hour before your start time until they leave.

6. Mark rooms you don't want photographed

Your Shine Report has photos of each room. If there's a room you'd rather keep private, mark it "no photos" and it won't be photographed.

7. Follow your clean on the day

The day before, you get a reminder and a quick checklist. On the day, you can see when your cleaner is on the way, has arrived, and is all done.

8. Check your Shine Report, and flag anything missed

After the clean, your Shine Report shows photos of each room. Missed a spot? Tap Flag a spot within 24 hours and we come back to fix it, free.

9. Message us and your cleaner in the app

Messages are the fastest way to reach us, and our replies show up right there. Messages with your cleaner stay in the app, so you both keep your contact details private.

10. Move a clean for free, and tip if you like

Need a different day? Move your clean free in the app up to 48 hours before it starts. Tips are optional: add one when you book or after the clean, and 100% goes to your cleaner.
