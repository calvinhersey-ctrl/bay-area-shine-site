The Bay Area Shine app is here

Bay Area Shine · October 2026 · 2 minute read

We've been building something we wish we'd had from day one: an app that makes booking a clean, and everything after it, simple.

Why an app?

Booking a cleaner usually means back-and-forth texts, guessing who will show up, and hoping it went well. The app puts it all in one place: you book, you see who's coming, you follow the clean, and you get photos when it's done.

What you can do in it

  • Book your clean and see the price before you pay.
  • Pick the day and the start time.
  • See who's coming before the day: first name, photo, rating and reviews.
  • Keep your door code in a locked field, never in a chat or a text.
  • Follow it on the day: on the way, arrived, all done.
  • Get your Shine Report, with photos of each room.
  • Flag anything missed within 24 hours, and we come back to fix it, free.
  • Message us and your cleaner in the app, with your contact details kept private.

Where to find it

Open the web app on any device. No download needed. An iPhone app on the App Store will follow.

Book in the app

Start with your price

Build your price on the home page first, then tap Book and the app picks up where you left off.
