Cleaning tips

Airbnb turnover cleaning checklist for hosts

Bay Area Shine · October 2026 · 2 minute read

A turnover is more than a clean. It's a reset, so the next guest walks into a place that feels new. Use this checklist for every changeover.

Linens and towels

  • Strip the beds and collect every towel, including the ones in odd places.
  • Remake the beds with fresh linens.
  • Lay out fresh towels the same way every time, so the place looks like your listing photos.

Kitchen

  • Counters, sink and stovetop.
  • Microwave, inside and out.
  • Clear out anything guests left in the fridge.
  • Run or empty the dishwasher and put everything back where guests expect it.

Bathrooms

  • Toilet, tub or shower, sink and mirrors.
  • Check floors and drains for hair.
  • Restock toilet paper and soap.

Bedrooms and living areas

  • Dust surfaces and wipe handles and switches.
  • Vacuum and mop the floors.
  • Check under beds and in drawers for anything left behind.
  • Reset furniture and pillows to match your listing.

Restock

  • Toilet paper, paper towels and soap.
  • Dish soap and trash bags.
  • Anything your listing promises, like coffee or tea.

Before you lock up

  • Take out all the trash.
  • Check for damage or missing items, and photograph anything you need to report.
  • Set the thermostat and lights for arrival.
  • Lock up, and change the door code between guests if you do.
