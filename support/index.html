Help and support

Email us: hello@bayareashinecleaners.com

We aim to reply within one business day. Safety reports are looked at within 24 hours, every day.

Email us

Using the app? The fastest way is Messages › Bay Area Shine in the app. Our replies show up right there.

Feel unsafe right now? Call 911. Cleaners: use Need help in the crew app.

When you email, please include

  • the email or mobile number on your account,
  • the day of the clean,
  • a photo if something's wrong.

Please never send a door code by email or chat. Use the locked code field in the app.

Customers

How do I book?

Open the app (or our website), choose your clean, pick a day and start time, and pay the $40 deposit. It comes off your total.

Can I move my clean?

Yes, free in the app up to 48 hours before it starts (24 hours with Shine Plus). Closer than that, message us.

What if I cancel?

The $40 deposit is kept and nothing else is charged. If no cleaner can take your clean, you get the $40 back or another time.

Who's coming?

You'll see your cleaner's profile before the day: name, photo, rating and reviews.

Something was missed.

Tap Flag a spot in your Shine Report within 24 hours (48 with Shine Plus), and we'll come back to fix it, free.

Something was damaged.

Tell us within 24 hours, with a photo if you can.

How do tips work?

Optional. Whatever you choose goes 100% to your cleaner. It's charged after the clean, and never if the clean doesn't happen.

How do I cancel my plan?

Account › My plan › Cancel plan: one tap, any time. Unused cleans are refunded.

How do I block or report someone?

On their profile or in the chat, choose Block or Report. They aren't told who reported them.

How do I delete my account?

Account › Privacy › Delete my account, or see how to delete your account.

Cleaners

How do I join?

Download Bay Area Shine Crew, make your profile, and we'll review it.

When am I paid?

Weekly, for every job you've finished. Tips are 100% yours, on top.

Can I drop a job?

Yes, free up to 48 hours before. Closer than that, ask the office in the app.

Not safe at a home?

Use Need help, or leave. You never have to go back, and you can block the customer.

Payout or tax form questions?

Me › Earnings, or message the office.
